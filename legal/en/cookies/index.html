VitaPass cookie policy

Version 1.0 · 8 October 2026 · Draft pending legal review. The Spanish version prevails.

1. Summary

VitaPass does not use cookies. Nor does it use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. That is why we do not ask you to accept cookies: there are none to accept.

This policy covers the VitaPass web pages: the viewer that opens emergency QR codes and stickers, the account deletion page and the professional console (web and desktop app). Controller: [PENDING: name or company name], tax ID [PENDING], [PENDING: contact email].

2. What your browser does store, and why it needs no consent

The law (art. 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002, LSSI, implementing the ePrivacy Directive) allows, without consent, what is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for. That is all we use:

  • QR viewer: a *service worker* keeps a copy of the page itself and its code (not of any data) so it opens without a connection. It stores nothing about the patient: the summary is decrypted in the browser's memory and is gone when the tab is closed.
  • Account deletion page: keeps your session in the browser's *sessionStorage* only while the tab is open.
  • Professional console: keeps the session and the decryption key in session storage, which is cleared when the window is closed.

None of this is used to track you or to know what you do on other websites, and none of it is shared.

3. Third parties

The public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit them, GitHub receives your IP address and the technical data of the connection in order to serve and protect the page, like any web host. GitHub sets no cookies on these pages. The pages can only connect to their own site and to our server in the European Union: the content security policy prevents anything else.

4. How to delete it

You can delete what your browser stores from its settings (website data). If you delete the viewer's copy, it will just not open offline the first time.

5. Changes

If we ever used cookies that require your consent, we would ask you before setting them, with a button to accept and an equally visible one to reject, and we would update this policy.
