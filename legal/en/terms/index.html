2. What VitaPass is and what it is not

VitaPass is a personal health record: you keep your health information in it (from public and private healthcare and from occupational health checks) and decide who you show it to, for example with a QR code that any health professional can read in their own language.

VitaPass is not:

  • Your official medical record. That is kept by your health service and your healthcare providers. VitaPass stores what you provide, which may be incomplete or out of date.
  • A medical or emergency service. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments or monitor your health. In an emergency, call 112 (or the emergency number of the country you are in). The SOS button always calls 112 first and does not replace it.
  • A medical device in its health record function. Any module that is one (for example, reading the home checks of an in vitro diagnostic device) will say so expressly, with its intended use and its marking.
